PRIVACY POLICY

DiveArt

Effective date: 25 September 2026

DiveArt is developed and published by Boris Hurinek.

Information DiveArt processes

DiveArt accesses only the photos and videos that the user allows it to access through the device's media permissions. DiveArt reads the media and associated information needed to display, process and save restored copies.

All colour restoration and compression are performed locally on the user's device. DiveArt does not upload or transmit photos, videos, media information or usage information to DiveArt-owned servers. It does not contain advertising, analytics or third-party behavioural tracking.

DiveArt stores application settings, processing and recovery information, and DiveArt Pro entitlement status locally on the user's device. DiveArt may also keep diagnostic logs in private application storage to help diagnose errors. These logs are not automatically transmitted.

If the user chooses to contact support, DiveArt may prepare non-personal diagnostic information about a failed processing job for inclusion in an email. This is sent only if the user chooses to send the email and is never transmitted automatically.

If the user purchases DiveArt Pro, purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. DiveArt receives only the purchase and entitlement information needed to confirm and restore DiveArt Pro access. DiveArt does not receive the user's payment card, bank account or other payment-method details.

Data processing and sharing

All photo and video processing is performed locally on the user's device. The user controls which media DiveArt can access through the device's media-access settings.

DiveArt does not sell personal information and does not share photos, videos, media information or usage information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Google Play may process account, purchase, subscription and payment information when the user views, purchases, restores or manages DiveArt Pro. That processing is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases and subscriptions

Prices and applicable purchase or subscription terms are shown in DiveArt and by Google Play before a purchase is confirmed. Recurring subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through Google Play.

DiveArt may contact Google Play to obtain available purchase information and localised prices, confirm the current entitlement, and restore previous purchases. DiveArt does not operate a separate user account or payment system for Android purchases.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DiveArt's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy takes effect.
