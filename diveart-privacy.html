PRIVACY POLICY

DiveArt

Effective date: 8 November 2026

DiveArt is developed and published by Boris Hurinek.

Photos and videos

DiveArt accesses photos, videos and related information with your permission to browse, restore and save media. Colour restoration and compression take place on your device. DiveArt does not upload your photos, videos or related media information to any server. DiveArt has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Local data and retention

DiveArt stores settings, processing and recovery information, DiveArt Pro plan status and diagnostic logs in private app storage on your device. Logs are not sent automatically. This local app data is removed when you uninstall DiveArt; restored photos and videos remain in your device's photo gallery. You can change media permissions in device settings.

Plans and subscriptions

The Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) handles payments and subscriptions, including account and payment information. DiveArt receives only the transaction and plan information needed to verify and restore your DiveArt Pro plan. We do not receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through the relevant store.

Data sharing and security

DiveArt does not sell your data or share your media or usage information for advertising or marketing. DiveArt's local data is held in private app storage. Apple and Google process store transactions under their respective privacy policies.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email borishurinekapps@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use the information you send to answer your enquiry. You can request deletion of information you have emailed us. We may update this policy if our practices change and will update the effective date.
